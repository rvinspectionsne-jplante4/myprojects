Fifth Wheel Trailers

✅ Fifth wheel trailers (also known as fivers) are a popular upgrade from a travel trailer.
Sporting most of the systems and amenities of the larger Class A motor home,
fivers are the popular choice of full-time travelers.

✅ The convenience of having the running gear (the tow vehicle) separate from the house (the camper)
means you can have regular vehicle maintenance performed without getting a hotel.

✅ If you already have a one-ton duallie pickup, consider a fifth wheel camper to fulfill your traveling dreams.
