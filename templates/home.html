Pre-purchase Inspections

Pre-purchase inspections are performed to identify issues with the camper you are about to buy. Problems like water infiltration, rodent activity and inoperable systems can cost thousands of dollars to repair and limit the amount of time you spend enjoying your camper.

A pre-purchase inspection includes:

Inspect roof for condition and adequate sealant around seams and penetrations

Inspect slide tops, toppers, mechanisms and seals

Inspect sidewalls for delamination

Inspect window seals and caulking

Inspect chassis, tires, underbelly and axles

Inspect holding tanks and piping

Inspect interior walls, ceilings and floors

Comprehensive moisture detection

Inspect 120 volt AC electrical, 12 volt DC electrical, generator, batteries, solar panels and inverter/converter/charger

Inspect city and onboard water systems for proper operation. Verify that the water heater produces hot water on all power sources

Run all galley appliances and check for proper operation. Check for propane leaks.

Verify that the refrigerator runs on all power sources
