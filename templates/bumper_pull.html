Travel Trailers

{% comment %} {% endcomment %}

🔶 Travel trailers (also known as bumper-pulls) are, for many campers, their first rig.
Ranging in size from 15 to 40 feet, the only limit is how big your tow vehicle is.

🔶 Inspection of a travel trailer will include the basic items and inspection of the running gear to ensure that the camper can be safely towed.

🔶 In addition, the trailer's gross vehicular weight and tongue weight should be compared to the tow vehicle's capacity.
